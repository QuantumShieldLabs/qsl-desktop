RULED: Cancel invite REVOKES, it does not merely delete a local record — the revoke_token kills the relay slot so a late redemption is impossible. When the relay is unreachable the local revoke still happens, the relay revoke is queued, and the copy says so plainly. The client already refuses a late redemption of a locally-revoked invite even if the relay lies.
